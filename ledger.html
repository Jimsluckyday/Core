"Picks" counts distinct calls (each parlay leg is its own pick, but the same call reused across several parlays only counts once); "Record" counts actual wagers (a whole parlay is one bet, win or lose, no matter how many legs) — for a heavy-parlay capper these numbers won't match, and that's expected, not an error. Picks can land either side of Record: more legs than wagers is normal for parlays, but a capper who builds many parlay slips from a small, reused pool of the same calls can show FEWER Picks than Record — hover the ⚠︎ next to Picks when that happens for the exact breakdown.
