⚠ Reduce your bet size on parlays

A parlay needs every leg to hit, so its real win probability is much lower than a single straight bet -- betting it at your normal size doesn't make sense given that reduced chance of winning.

To reflect that, every dollar total shown for parlays on this site -- including the "wins $X" figure on this card and the site-wide running total -- is calculated at 25% of our normal $100 unit ($25), not the full $100 used for straight bets.

When you place a parlay yourself, size it the same way: about a quarter of what you'd normally bet on a straight pick.
